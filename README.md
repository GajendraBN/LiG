## App Store Description (4000 characters max)

**Leben in Deutschland** is your comprehensive guide to starting a new life in Germany. Whether you're moving for work, study, or family, this app provides everything you need to navigate German bureaucracy, culture, and daily life.

**LEARN & UNDERSTAND**
Master 12 essential topics with our interactive learning modules:
- Anmeldung (Registration) - Your first and most important step
- Health Insurance - Public vs private, how to choose
- Broadcasting Fee (Rundfunkbeitrag) - What it is and how to pay
- Church Tax - Understanding and opting out
- Renting an Apartment - Finding housing and tenant rights
- Quiet Hours (Ruhezeiten) - Noise rules you need to know
- Recycling & Pfand - The German waste system explained
- Public Transport - From tickets to the Deutschland-Ticket
- Sunday Shopping - What's open and when
- Public Holidays - Federal and state celebrations
- Driving & License - Converting your license
- German Etiquette - Social norms and cultural tips

**TEST YOUR KNOWLEDGE**
- 120+ quiz questions based on real integration test content
- Track your progress with detailed statistics
- Earn XP and level up from Newcomer to Eingebürgert
- Maintain your learning streak

**90+ CURATED RESOURCES**
Hand-picked free websites and apps across 15 categories:
- Learn German (VHS Lernportal, Deutsche Welle, Duolingo)
- Jobs & Career (Arbeitsagentur, LinkedIn, XING)
- Housing (ImmobilienScout24, WG-Gesucht)
- Finance & Banking (N26, DKB, SCHUFA)
- Health (Doctolib, TK, AOK)
- Government (Make it in Germany, BAMF, Handbook Germany)
- And 9 more categories!

**GAMIFICATION**
- XP points for learning and quizzes
- 5 levels: Newcomer → Resident → Local → Expert → Eingebürgert
- Daily streaks to keep you motivated
- Badges for achievements

**WHY THIS APP?**
- Created by expats, for expats
- All official German government sources verified
- Bilingual explanations (German terms with English descriptions)
- 100% free, no subscriptions
- Works offline for essential content
- Regular updates with new content

**PERFECT FOR:**
- New arrivals to Germany
- Expats and international workers
- Students studying in Germany
- Anyone preparing for the Einbürgerungstest
- People curious about German culture

Start your journey to feeling at home in Germany today!
